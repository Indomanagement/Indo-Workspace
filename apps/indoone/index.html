Features

What Indoone can do

A conversational AI assistant — and a lot more. Here's what's inside Indoone today.

AI chat

Ask anything and get clear, useful answers in a familiar chat. Attach a camera shot, a photo or a file for extra context — or use voice input when it is easier to speak.

Vibe — live voice

Have a real, back-and-forth spoken conversation in real time — live audio both ways, with mute and speaker controls, so it feels like talking to someone rather than typing. Works in English and Indian languages.

Memory

Indoone remembers the details that matter over time. Review, edit or delete anything it has stored, and keep your personal profile separate from what the assistant remembers.

Authenticator

A built-in TOTP authenticator for your two-factor codes — add accounts by scanning a QR code, pasting a URI or entering a setup key, with backup and a printable recovery PDF.

Plugins

Bring your own tools into the chat — connect Gmail, Zoho Mail and GitHub so Indoone can work with your mail and code where you already are.

Security

Keep your chats and codes private with an app lock and biometric unlock, plus screenshot protection that stops sensitive screens from being captured.

Cloud sync

One Indoone account across every device — your chats and memory stay in sync, so you can pick up exactly where you left off.

Built for India

Designed around Indian users and how they actually work, from the ground up — Indian context first, not bolted on afterwards.
